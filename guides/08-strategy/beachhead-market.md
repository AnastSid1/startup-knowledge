# Beachhead Market

> A beachhead market is the small, specific market segment you focus on first to win decisively before expanding.

## What it means

Borrowed from military strategy and popularized in startup teaching (including *Crossing the Chasm* and Disciplined Entrepreneurship), a beachhead is your initial battlefield:

- narrow enough to dominate
- valuable enough to matter
- connected enough to expand from

It pairs closely with your [ICP](../02-customers/icp.md) and [wedge](../01-product/wedge.md).

## Why it matters

Startups lose by attacking continents with a canoe. A beachhead creates:

- clearer product requirements
- denser word of mouth
- reference customers that look alike
- a credible “we win here” story for hiring and fundraising

Winning a beachhead is often the practical path toward larger [SAM](../03-market/sam.md) and [TAM](../03-market/tam.md).

## When founders should care

- Choosing the first segment
- When early customers are scattered and learning is noisy
- Planning expansion adjacencies after PMF in the beachhead
- Designing GTM messaging for one audience

## How it works

Beachhead selection criteria:

1. **Urgency:** acute pain
2. **Reachability:** you can access buyers
3. **Homogeneity:** similar needs and language
4. **Willingness to pay**
5. **Word-of-mouth density**
6. **Expansion bridges** to adjacent markets

### Expansion pattern

```text
Win dental clinics in one region
 → dental clinic chains nationally
   → veterinary clinics (similar scheduling/compliance patterns)
```

Each move should reuse product, distribution, or brand assets.

## Practical example

A cybersecurity startup dreams of “protecting all mid-market IT environments.”

Beachhead: **50–200 person US law firms using Microsoft 365**, sold via IT providers that already serve law offices.

Why it works:

- shared compliance fears
- similar stack
- referenceability within legal networks
- partner channel access

After owning that niche, expand to accounting firms with similar partners — not to every industry at once.

## Common confusion

| Term | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| Niche forever | Beachhead can be temporary by design |
| TAM | Long-term opportunity; beachhead is the start |
| Wedge | Product/entry angle; beachhead is the market segment focus |
| SOM | Numeric capture target; beachhead is strategic focus |
