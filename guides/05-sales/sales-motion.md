# Sales Motion

> A sales motion is the repeatable pattern of how customers buy from you — self-serve, sales-assisted, sales-led, or hybrid.

## What it means

Your sales motion defines who does the work of conversion and when humans get involved.

| Motion | How buyers start | When humans help |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-serve / PLG | Signup and purchase alone | Rarely; support maybe |
| Product-led sales | Product usage first | Sales engages PQLs |
| Sales-assisted | Trial + light touch | SDRs/AEs guide mid-funnel |
| Sales-led | Demo/request first | Sales owns end-to-end |
| Enterprise motion | RFP / security review | SE, legal, procurement |

Many companies run different motions for different segments.

## Why it matters

Wrong motion burns money:

- $30/mo product with full enterprise sales → CAC catastrophe
- $100k ACV product with only a help widget → stalled deals

Motion choice also shapes product requirements (self-serve onboarding vs demo environments, SSO, etc.).

## When founders should care

- Packaging and pricing decisions
- First GTM hires
- Expanding from SMB to mid-market/enterprise
- Designing [PLG](../01-product/product-led-growth.md) vs sales-led org structure

## How it works

Choose motion using:

1. ACV and margin
2. Complexity of setup
3. Buyer committee size
4. Frequency of purchase
5. Need for trust/security review

### Hybrid example

```text
Free/team self-serve → PQL threshold → AE for annual + SSO → CS for expansion
```

### Transition risks

Moving upmarket without enterprise readiness (security, roles, SLA, procurement collateral) creates failed pilots. Moving downmarket with heavy sales creates inefficient CAC.

## Practical example

A analytics startup:

- SMB: credit card self-serve, $200/mo
- Mid-market: sales-assisted at 10+ seats
- Enterprise: sales-led with SSO, MSA, and dedicated onboarding

They maintain one product with feature gates, not three disconnected products — but motions differ by segment.

## Common confusion

| Term | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| GTM | Broader system; motion is the conversion pattern |
| Sales methodology | SPIN/MEDDICC etc. are conversation frameworks inside a motion |
| Channel | Where leads come from; motion is how they buy |
