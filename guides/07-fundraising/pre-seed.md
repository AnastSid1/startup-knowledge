# Pre-Seed

> Pre-seed is the earliest institutional (or angel-heavy) financing stage, usually funding team formation, problem discovery, and an initial product wedge.

## What it means

Pre-seed capital helps founders go from idea/prototype toward early evidence:

- recruiting a founding team
- building an [MVP](../01-product/mvp.md)
- talking to [ICP](../02-customers/icp.md) customers
- finding initial distribution

Round mechanics are often [SAFE](safe.md)/convertible instruments, sometimes small priced rounds. Check sizes and norms vary widely by city, year, and sector.

## Why it matters

Pre-seed buys learning time. It is not usually a scale round. The job is to reduce the biggest risks: team, problem urgency, and early product value.

## When founders should care

- When personal runway cannot fund enough experiments
- When a small amount of capital unlocks a critical hire or build
- Before claiming you need a large Seed without evidence

Some teams skip formal pre-seed and raise a Seed directly — depends on traction and networks.

## How it works

Typical narrative ingredients (illustrative, not requirements):

- strong [founder-market fit](../01-product/founder-market-fit.md)
- crisp problem and wedge
- early user signal (even qualitative)
- plan for what the round unlocks in 12–18 months

Investors underwrite the team and insight more than polished metrics.

## Practical example

Two technical founders raise a modest pre-seed on a SAFE to:

1. quit part-time consulting
2. ship MVP to 25 design partners
3. find whether 10 retain weekly and 5 pay

Success looks like evidence worthy of a Seed — not $2M ARR.

## Common confusion

| Term | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| Friends & family | Informal early capital; may precede pre-seed |
| Seed | Usually larger, expects clearer traction path |
| Grant / accelerator money | Different instrument and expectations |
