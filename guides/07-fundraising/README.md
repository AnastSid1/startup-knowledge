# Fundraising & Company Stages

This section explains how startup financing typically works — stages, instruments, ownership, and key documents.

> Educational overview, not legal or investment advice. Cap tables, SAFEs, and term sheets have legal consequences — use qualified counsel.

## Stage path (simplified)

```text
Pre-Seed → Seed → Series A → Series B → later rounds / exit
                 ↘ Bridge rounds may appear between stages
```
