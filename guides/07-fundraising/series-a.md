# Series A

> Series A is typically the first major growth financing round after a company has evidence of product-market fit and a repeatable way to acquire customers.

## What it means

While definitions blur, Series A investors often look for:

- clearer [PMF](../01-product/product-market-fit.md) signals
- repeatable GTM motion (not only founder heroics)
- meaningful retention
- efficient-enough growth path
- a team that can scale departments

Series A is usually a priced equity round with a lead investor, board seat dynamics, and a full [term sheet](term-sheet.md).

Exact ARR thresholds marketed on Twitter are unreliable — they vary by year, sector, geography, and capital climate.

## Why it matters

Series A capital is meant to scale what works: hire go-to-market, strengthen product, expand market presence. Raising A before repeatability often creates a painful “Series A crunch.”

## When founders should care

- When Seed milestones around retention and repeatability are in sight
- When runway requires starting the process months ahead
- When you can show a coherent scaling plan and use of funds

## How it works

Common process:

1. tighten metrics narrative (growth, retention, cohorts, margins)
2. prepare materials and data room
3. meet leads; create thoughtful urgency (without gimmicks)
4. term sheet → diligence → close
5. update [cap table](cap-table.md); install governance

### What changes after A

- board meetings become more formal
- hiring accelerates
- expectations for process and forecasting rise
- competitive intensity may rise as you become more visible

## Practical example

A B2B SaaS company raises Series A with:

- strong ICP retention
- net revenue retention >100%
- proven inbound + sales-assisted hybrid motion
- plan to hire AEs and expand one adjacency

They are not yet default-alive, but unit economics suggest a path with scale.

## Common confusion

| Term | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| Large Seed | May overlap in size; A usually priced with lead + different expectations |
| Series B | Scaling a working machine across segments/geos |
| Growth metrics vanity | A buyers scrutinize retention quality |
