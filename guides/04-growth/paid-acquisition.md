# Paid Acquisition

> Paid acquisition means buying access to potential customers through ads, sponsorships, paid partnerships, or lead purchases.

## What it means

You pay a channel to put your offer in front of people. Common channels:

- search ads (high intent)
- social/product ads
- sponsored newsletters
- paid app install campaigns
- affiliate/influencer fees
- paid event booths

Paid acquisition is fuel. It works best when the engine — product value, conversion, retention — already runs.

## Why it matters

Paid channels can accelerate learning and growth when [unit economics](../08-strategy/unit-economics.md) work. They can also create a mirage of traction that collapses when spend stops.

Used well, paid acquisition is a controllable dial. Used early with weak retention, it is an expensive way to learn the wrong lesson (“we can buy users”).

## When founders should care

- After early conversion and retention signals look decent
- When organic channels are too slow for a time-sensitive goal
- When you can measure CAC, payback, and cohort LTV
- When testing messaging quickly with small budgets

Be cautious pre-[PMF](../01-product/product-market-fit.md).

## How it works

Core loop:

```text
Spend → clicks/leads → activated users → paying customers → LTV
```

Must-track metrics:

- CPC / CPM
- CAC (see [CAC](../06-finance/cac.md))
- [conversion rate](../06-finance/conversion-rate.md) by funnel stage
- [payback period](../06-finance/payback-period.md)
- cohort retention of paid users

### Simple viability check

```text
If CAC = $400 and monthly gross profit / customer = $80,
payback = 5 months (directionally okay for many SaaS models).
If retention is poor, LTV collapses and $400 is too much.
```

## Practical example

A B2B tool runs Google ads on “hubspot onboarding checklist.”

- CPC $12
- landing → trial 20%
- trial → paid 15%
- customers from ads: CAC ≈ $12 / (0.20 × 0.15) = $400
- ARPU $120/mo, margin 85% → $102 monthly GP
- payback ≈ 4 months

If paid users churn 2× faster than organic users, revisit targeting or onboarding before scaling spend.

## Common confusion

| Term | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| Growth | Paid is one input, not growth itself |
| Performance marketing | Discipline inside paid acquisition |
| Organic | Not directly paying per click/lead |
| Brand spend | Harder to attribute; different goal |
