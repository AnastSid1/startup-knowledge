# Organic Growth

> Organic growth is new demand that arrives without paying directly for each click or lead — through word of mouth, content, brand, virality, community, and product-driven discovery.

## What it means

Organic does not mean “free” in total cost. You still invest in product quality, content, community, SEO, and brand. It means you are not primarily buying distribution via ads or paid lead gen for every customer.

Common organic engines:

- word of mouth / referrals
- SEO and educational content
- open source
- community and events
- [viral loops](viral-loops.md)
- marketplace ranking
- brand direct traffic

## Why it matters

Organic growth usually improves [unit economics](../08-strategy/unit-economics.md) and resilience. When ad costs spike or platforms change, organic engines can keep compounding.

Many top SaaS companies blend organic foundation with selective paid amplification.

## When founders should care

- Always — especially before paid scale
- When CAC in paid channels is rising
- When building [PLG](../01-product/product-led-growth.md) motions
- When your category has strong search or community behavior

## How it works

Pick organic bets that match buyer behavior:

| If buyers... | Organic bet |
| --- | --- |
| Search for solutions | SEO / docs / comparisons |
| Ask peers | Community presence, referrals |
| Share artifacts | Templates, public pages, invites |
| Assemble tools | Integrations marketplace |

Measure organic quality, not only traffic: activation and retention of organic cohorts vs paid cohorts.

## Practical example

A developer API company:

- excellent docs rank for problem queries
- sample apps get forked
- engineers share dashboard screenshots
- unpaid signups convert at 4%, retain well

They later add paid acquisition for specific high-intent keywords — using organic as the base layer.

## Common confusion

| Term | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| Free growth | Still costs time and salary |
| Virality | Subset of organic mechanisms |
| Inbound marketing | Often content-led organic, not the whole category |
| Luck | Real organic systems are designed and measured |
