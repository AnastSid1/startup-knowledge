# Positioning

> Positioning is the deliberate choice of how your product should be understood relative to alternatives in the mind of a target customer.

## What it means

Positioning answers:

- what you are
- who it is for
- what job it does
- why it is the best choice versus alternatives

Alternatives include competitors, manual workarounds, spreadsheets, agencies, and “do nothing.”

Strong positioning is specific. Weak positioning sounds like: “an AI-powered platform that helps businesses unlock efficiency.”

## Why it matters

Positioning shapes messaging, product packaging, sales narratives, pricing comparisons, and even roadmap priorities. If customers miscategorize you, they evaluate you with the wrong checklist and you lose.

April Dunford’s work is popular among startups because it treats positioning as a practical system, not a slogan workshop.

## When founders should care

- When prospects say “we already have something like that” incorrectly
- When sales cycles feel chaotic
- When you expand into a new segment
- When preparing website homepage and pitch narrative

Revisit positioning after major product or ICP shifts.

## How it works

A practical sequence:

1. List true competitive alternatives customers compare you against.
2. Identify unique attributes you actually have.
3. Map those attributes to value for a specific [ICP](../02-customers/icp.md).
4. Choose the market category that makes your strengths obvious.
5. Express it in clear customer language.
6. Test in sales calls and onboarding copy.

### Positioning canvas (lightweight)

| Element | Example |
| --- | --- |
| For | Shopify DTC Ops leaders |
| Who struggle with | returns chaos across tools |
| Our product is | returns triage software |
| That | auto-classifies and routes refunds/exchanges |
| Unlike | shared inboxes + spreadsheets |
| We | cut handling time 40% in week one |

## Practical example

A database company can position as:

- “faster Postgres” (comparison category: databases)
- “analytics warehouse alternative” (different buyers/checklist)
- “embedded database for IoT devices” (narrower beachhead)

Same technology family, different positioning, different GTM.

If your win rate jumps when you say “we replace the returns spreadsheet for Shopify Ops,” you found sharper positioning than “AI operations platform.”

## Common confusion

| Term | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| Messaging | Words that express positioning |
| Branding | Broader identity system; positioning is competitive meaning |
| Wedge | Strategic entry; positioning communicates the entry |
| Tagline | Can reflect positioning; is not the whole thing |
