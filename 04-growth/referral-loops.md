# Referral Loops

> A referral loop is a growth system where existing users intentionally recommend the product to new users, often motivated by incentives, status, or reciprocity — and those new users later refer others.

## What it means

Referrals are explicit recommendations. They may be:

- incentivized (credits, cash, free months)
- unincentivized (pure word of mouth)
- dual-sided (reward giver and receiver)
- single-sided (reward only one party)

A referral *loop* exists when referred users become referrers at a meaningful rate.

## Why it matters

Referred customers often have:

- lower CAC
- higher trust at entry
- better conversion
- sometimes better retention (depends on fit)

Referral programs can amplify products people already love. They rarely fix products people do not.

## When founders should care

- After users already recommend you informally
- When economics support rewards without breaking margins
- When your ICP is socially connected
- Not as a substitute for [PMF](../01-product/product-market-fit.md)

## How it works

```text
Happy user → referral action → referred prospect converts → reward delivered → new user reaches happiness → refers again
```

Design checklist:

1. Ask at a moment of delight, not annoyance
2. Make sharing easy
3. Align incentive with product value (credits > ugly cash spam, often)
4. Prevent fraud
5. Measure referred cohort quality

### Simple economics

If reward costs $40 total and referred CAC equivalent is $40 plus friction, ensure [LTV](../06-finance/ltv.md) and margins still work versus other channels.

## Practical example

A consumer fintech gives $20 to referrer and $20 to referee after the referee’s first qualified transaction.

Results only work if:

- first transaction activation is strong
- fraud is controlled
- referred users retain
- contribution margin after rewards remains healthy

If users refer strangers who cash out and leave, you built a bounty scheme, not a growth loop.

## Common confusion

| Term | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| Viral loop | Often intrinsic product exposure; referral is intentional recommendation |
| Affiliate program | Typically broader partners, more commercial publishing |
| NPS | Sentiment metric; not a loop |
| Word of mouth | Organic referrals without formal program |
