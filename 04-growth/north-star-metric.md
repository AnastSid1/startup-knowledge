# North Star Metric

> A North Star Metric is the single primary measure that best captures the value your product delivers to customers and the growth of that value over time.

## What it means

The North Star is not “vanity revenue at all costs.” It is a product-growth metric that aligns teams around customer value.

Examples:

| Company type | Possible North Star |
| --- | --- |
| Collaboration SaaS | Weekly active teams completing shared docs |
| Marketplace | Weekly successful transactions |
| Education app | Learners completing a weekly study session |
| Billing API | Payment volume successfully processed |

Revenue still matters. The North Star helps you grow revenue *for the right reasons*.

## Why it matters

Without a North Star, teams optimize conflicting KPIs: marketing celebrates signups while product celebrates feature launches and finance celebrates short-term revenue — even if customer value is flat.

A good North Star creates alignment and prioritization.

## When founders should care

- When the team is large enough to need shared focus
- When growth work becomes cross-functional
- After you understand the action that predicts retention and value
- Not as an excuse to ignore financial metrics

## How it works

Choose a metric that is:

1. **Value-aligned** — reflects customer success
2. **Responsive** — moves when product/growth work works
3. **Understandable** — teams can influence it
4. **Leading enough** — not only lagging accounting outcomes

Support it with input metrics: activation rate, retention, invite rate, conversion, etc.

### Anti-examples

- raw signups (too top-of-funnel)
- downloads (weak if inactive)
- pageviews (often vanity)
- total registered users (cumulative vanity)

## Practical example

A project management tool initially tracks MAU. Many MAUs log in once and leave.

They switch North Star to **“number of teams with ≥3 members completing ≥5 core actions per week.”**

This focuses the company on collaboration depth — which correlates with retention and expansion — instead of empty signups.

## Common confusion

| Term | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| OKR key result | May include the North Star, plus other outcomes |
| One metric that matters (OMTM) | Similar lean-startup idea; North Star often more durable |
| Revenue | Critical business metric; may lag customer value |
| Funnel metrics | Inputs to the North Star system |
