# Sales

> Sales is the process of helping a qualified customer understand value, resolve concerns, and buy — then expand — in a way that works for both sides.

## What it means

In startups, sales often begins as founder-led conversations. Later it becomes a team with stages, tooling, and quotas.

Core jobs of sales:

- find or receive demand
- qualify fit
- discover pain and decision process
- demonstrate value
- handle objections
- close
- hand off to onboarding/success
- expand accounts

Sales is not “talking people into things they do not need.” Bad-fit sales create churn and support pain.

## Why it matters

For many B2B products, sales is the difference between interesting usage and revenue. Even [PLG](../01-product/product-led-growth.md) companies often need sales for larger accounts.

Learning from sales calls also improves product and positioning faster than analytics alone.

## When founders should care

- From first paying customer conversations
- When ACV requires human trust and customization
- When hiring first AE/SDR
- When pipeline becomes the growth constraint

If you are fully self-serve and converting well, keep sales light until account complexity demands it.

## How it works

### Simple early-stage pipeline

```text
Lead → Qualified → Demo/Discovery → Proposal → Closed-won / Closed-lost
```

Track:

- stage conversion rates
- sales cycle length
- win rate by segment
- average ACV
- reasons lost

### Founder-led sales playbook (lightweight)

1. Only talk to ICP prospects
2. Spend more time on discovery than pitching
3. Quantify [product worth](../01-product/product-worth.md)
4. Multi-thread: user + economic buyer
5. Write down objections and turn them into FAQ/product work
6. Ask for the close or next clear step

## Practical example

A seed-stage SaaS founder runs 12 demos/week.

Findings:

- win rate 25% when prospect has an active painful workaround
- win rate 5% when prospect is “exploring AI”
- average close time 21 days for SMB, 90 days for enterprises

Decision: reject non-urgent exploratory calls, rebuild outbound around trigger events, delay enterprise pursuit. Revenue quality improves even if top-of-funnel shrinks.

## Common confusion

| Term | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| Marketing | Creates awareness/demand; sales converts complex demand |
| Customer success | Post-sale value realization and retention |
| Business development | Partnerships / inorganic paths; overlaps but distinct |
| Manipulation | Short-term closes that destroy trust |
