# Go-To-Market (GTM)

> Go-to-market is the plan for how you reach, convince, convert, and expand a specific set of customers for a specific offer.

## What it means

GTM connects product, messaging, channels, and sales motion into one system:

- who we sell to ([ICP](../02-customers/icp.md))
- what we sell (offer, packaging, price)
- why us ([positioning](../08-strategy/positioning.md))
- where we reach them ([distribution](../04-growth/distribution.md))
- how we convert them ([sales motion](sales-motion.md))
- how we expand them after

GTM is not only a launch checklist. It is an ongoing operating system.

## Why it matters

Many products fail commercially with decent product quality because GTM is fuzzy: wrong buyer, wrong channel, wrong motion for the price point.

A clear GTM reduces wasted spend and shortens the path to repeatable revenue.

## When founders should care

- Before scaling spend or hiring a sales team
- When launching a new product or segment
- At fundraising, when explaining how capital becomes growth
- Whenever win rates are low and cycles are chaotic

## How it works

### GTM design sequence

1. Define beachhead ICP and use case
2. Clarify value promise and alternatives
3. Choose motion: self-serve, sales-led, or hybrid
4. Pick 1–2 primary channels (not ten)
5. Define funnel stages and conversion targets
6. Instrument metrics: CAC, win rate, cycle length, activation, expansion
7. Iterate weekly from call notes and funnel data

### Matching ACV to motion (illustrative)

| Typical ACV | Common motion |
| --- | --- |
| < $1k | Self-serve / light touch |
| $1k–$10k | Product-led + inside sales assist |
| $10k–$50k | Inside sales |
| $50k+ | Sales-led, often with solutions engineering |

Ranges vary by market; the principle is economic fit.

## Practical example

Product: AI QA for ecommerce support.

GTM v1:

- ICP: Shopify brands $10M–$60M GMV
- Offer: $500–$1,500/mo, 14-day trial
- Channel: partner agencies + targeted outbound to Heads of Support
- Motion: founder-led demos, then AE hire
- Expansion: add seats/macros packs after 60 days

They explicitly postpone enterprise procurement until the mid-market motion works.

## Common confusion

| Term | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| Marketing plan | Part of GTM, not the whole |
| Launch | Event; GTM is continuous |
| Sales playbook | Tactical conversion system inside GTM |
| Growth strategy | Overlaps; GTM emphasizes customer acquisition/conversion system for an offer |
